What sets us apart

Our exclusive solutions

More than partnerships — a cutting-edge platform that streamlines the working lives of experts and employers worldwide.

Expert Search Application

Our copyrighted application closes the gap between those seeking services and those offering them.

  • Automatic translation makes it easy to understand different languages during communication and transactions.
  • Instant service search for any manual labour or skill you are looking for.
  • Easy search shows the expert closest to your location.
  • Full details — phone number, type of expertise, and guarantee status.

Our goal: simplify finding the right expert in your vicinity, quickly and reliably.

Strategic Alliances with Embassies

We maintain special relationships with embassies of every country.

  • Embassy partnerships let us facilitate the movement of workers and simplify recruiting professionals from one country to another.

AI-Powered Verification

Our application leverages Artificial Intelligence to protect quality and trust.

  • Vetting: analyse and verify every individual to confirm the accuracy of their data and expertise.
  • Security: ensure our professionals meet all quality and professional standards.

Agents & Coordinators

A unique on-the-ground structure for reliable, person-to-person service.

  • Six agents per LGA in every Local Government Area across Nigeria.
  • Problem resolution: agents resolve issues, mediate conflicts, and receive complaints directly.
  • State coordinators supervise and manage all agents within their state.

Goal: expand this system of six agents per LGA, managed by a state coordinator, across the entire globe.

S MAHI Token Wallet

A dedicated payment system to simplify transactions worldwide.

  • Easy payroll: pay workers easily and seamlessly in any country globally.

Training & Government Connections

Skills development and access to essential institutions.

  • Vocational training helps unskilled individuals become certified professionals.
  • Institutional search locates the nearest government authorities, security agencies, hospitals, or schools with contact numbers.
