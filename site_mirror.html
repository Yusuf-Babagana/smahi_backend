📱 Our Expert Search Application

This is our Copyrighted application, designed to close the gap between those seeking services and those offering them.

  • Automatic Translation: Our App features Automatic Translation technology, which makes it easy to understand different languages globally during communication and transactions.
  • Instant Service Search: Whatever manual labor (handicraft) or skill you are looking for, you can directly access the Application.
  • Easy Search: Perform your search, and the App will show you the expert closest to your location.
  • Full Details: You will see their phone number, type of expertise, and their guarantee status (whether they provide a guarantee or not).

💡 Our Goal: To simplify the process of finding the right expert in your vicinity, quickly and reliably.

🤝 Strategic Alliances with Embassies

We maintain a special relationship with:

  • Embassies of Every Country: This partnership allows us to easily facilitate the movement of workers and simplify the process of recruiting professionals from one country to another.

🧠 Verification via AI Technology

Our application leverages Artificial Intelligence (AI) technology to:

  • Vetting: Analyze and verify every individual on our platform to confirm the accuracy of their data and expertise.
  • Security: Ensure that our professionals meet all our quality and professional standards.

👥 Our Agents and Coordinators – On-the-Ground Support

To ensure reliable, person-to-person service, we have a unique organizational structure:

  • Agents per Local Government (LGA): In every Local Government Area across Nigeria, we have six (6) trained Agents.
  • Problem Resolution: These Agents are responsible for resolving issues, mediating conflicts, and receiving complaints directly.
  • State Coordinators: In addition to the agents, we have one Coordinator in every state. This Coordinator is responsible for supervising and managing all the Agents operating within their respective state.

🎯 Goal: We aim to expand this system of six Agents per LGA, managed by a State Coordinator, across the entire globe.

💰 S MAHI Token Wallet – Seamless Global Payment

We have introduced a dedicated payment system to simplify transactions worldwide:

  • Easy Payroll: Our S MAHI Token Wallet provides the ability to pay workers easily and seamlessly in any country globally.

📚 Skills Training and Government Connections

  • Vocational Training: For individuals without a skill who wish to join our platform, we can provide them with vocational training to become certified professionals.
  • Institutional Search: If you perform a search, you can also locate the nearest Government Authorities, Police/Security Agencies, Hospitals, or Schools, along with their contact numbers.
