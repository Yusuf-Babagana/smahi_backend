## Key platform features

- Identity verification: all artisans and service providers undergo background
  and field identity verification by dedicated agents to guarantee security
  and quality control.
- Tiered client system: a Normal Client plan (free, limited directory
  browsing) and a premium VIP Client plan (unlocks full communication,
  continuous usage, and a golden badge that filters out non-serious traffic).
- Platform roles: Hire Artisans (client), Work as an Artisan, Field Agents,
  LGA Admins, State Coordinators, and a dedicated portal for
  businesses/companies to set up custom store dashboards.
- Automatic translation: chats are translated in real time between languages
  (including English and Hausa).
- Instant live chat between clients and artisans.
- Smart AI assistant (this assistant): multilingual (Hausa and English),
  recommends artisans and businesses to clients 24/7.
- Autonomous merchant negotiation (premium): premium users can activate a
  bargaining sub-AI that negotiates trades, locks inventory to prevent
  double-sales, and dispatches asset pickup locations.
